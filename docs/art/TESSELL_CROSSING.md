# Tessell crossing menu artwork

The main menu uses an original illustration generated with the built-in image-generation tool.
The image is decorative; title, menu choices and labels remain accessible HTML.

- Source: [tessell-crossing-source.png](tessell-crossing-source.png)
- Game asset: [tessell-crossing-menu.webp](../../src/assets/art/tessell-crossing-menu.webp)
- Encoding: WebP quality 88, original dimensions retained. No new runtime dependency.
- Integration: imported from `src/assets/art`, so the standalone build embeds it and needs no external image request.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original widescreen background key art for Ironmuster, a colourful, highly readable tactical mech game main menu. Create one polished 16:9 landscape illustration at 2048x1152 or larger, with absolutely no text, logos, UI, letters, symbols or watermark.

Scene: Tessell, a weathered frontier of sweeping terracotta cliffs, muted golden grass, jade-green valleys and turquoise water, a freight bridge crossing the valley, small repair outposts integrated into the landscape. Golden late-afternoon light, pale warm haze, dark blue-green foreground shadows.

Main subject at right two-thirds: a tough, squat Linewrought industrial walking war machine seen in three-quarter rear view looking into the valley, clearly bipedal with huge rectangular feet, thick piston legs, low broad torso, a recessed cyan rectangular cockpit, asymmetrical bolted rust-orange and cream armour plates, exposed dark mechanical joints, one short heavy cannon integrated beside the torso and a simple shoulder launcher box. Its design is practical salvaged workshop machinery, no human-like face, no humanoid helmet.

Far away near the bridge: one small, elegant ivory Aurelian walker with tall clean rectangular armour, deep blue joints and a narrow cyan sensor slit; suggest tension through distance rather than an active fight.

Style: sophisticated graphic expedition illustration, clean angular shapes, restrained painterly texture, crisp readable silhouettes, bold limited colour blocks, evocative environmental depth. It should match an original stylized indie strategy game, not photorealism, not a military shooter, not gritty dystopia, not a recognizable existing robot franchise.

Composition: the main mech entirely inside rightmost 55 percent of frame; expansive calm darker teal shadow and simple terrain in leftmost 40 percent for an overlaid menu, avoid details or bright sky in lower-left menu area; scenic horizon across upper third and strong diagonal leading toward the distant crossing. Keep bright warm scenery and mech silhouette beautifully framed at right. Painted environment with precise industrial construction, no speed blur or explosions. This is artwork only; the title and buttons will be real HTML placed over it.

# A Clean Account — missions A5-A8

Continuation of [AURELIAN_SCRIPT.md](./AURELIAN_SCRIPT.md).

## A5 — Quarry Receipt

**Why this battle happens:** Blackglass cut the replacement plates and holds the
only receipts connecting altered serials to working repair yards.

**Why it opens A6:** The essential ledger identifies a live conduit serving the
disputed yards, making their present dependence measurable rather than claimed.

**Briefing**

Secure the weigh ledger and preserve it through the copy. A second exposed site
contains the lift manifest and serial punch; it is optional but strengthens the
record and releases escort equipment. The quarry rewards long-range cover.
Local flankers know the short cuts through the dust.

**Opening exchange**

- Impulse / Arne: “A serial punch and false plates. At last, an uncomplicated
  entry.”
- Observer / Silas: “The punch proves alteration. The ledger will tell us what
  the alteration served.”
- Dispatch fallback: “Secure the weigh ledger. The lift manifest and serial
  punch are optional evidence.”

**Field exchange — essential ledger copied**

- Lead / Petra: “Receipts name operating yards and a shared power conduit. We
  have evidence of dependence, not a hidden production line.”
- Practical / Teodor: “Then the next seizure has a light switch.”

**Aftermath: The people in the account**

The ledger traces copied numbers to working repair yards rather than a secret
walker factory. It names their live power conduit and the shifts that depend on
it. The next warrant covers that line. The detachment still has to secure it;
it now knows what will stop working if export proceeds.

## A6 — Conduit Injunction

**Why this battle happens:** The Authority must verify which active machines are
covered by the altered records, and the live conduit is the only common service
point.

**Why it opens A7:** The conduit record names Barrow as keeper of both the master
warrant and the local maintenance compact.

**Briefing**

Secure the main isolator, then hold it while the clerks verify the live service
record. The line must go cold without destroying its machinery. A side bypass
can preserve power to the workshop block; holding it is optional and the
debrief will record the outcome. Expect one counterattack after verification
begins.

**Opening exchange**

- Lead / Petra: “Main isolator first. The side bypass stays live only if we can
  afford to hold it.”
- Practical / Teodor: “One real position and one choice. Better than four
  circles pretending to be a plan.”
- Dispatch fallback: “Secure the main isolator through verification. The
  workshop bypass is optional.”

**Field exchange — verification starts**

- Observer / Silas: “Every disputed yard appears on the load. Barrow holds the
  warrant and the compact that allowed it.”
- Impulse / Arne: “Then Barrow gives us the proper disposition.”

**Aftermath: One remaining signature**

The conduit record is verified. If the bypass was held, the workshop block
keeps power; otherwise it closes safely while the line is isolated. Neither
outcome changes the evidence. Barrow holds the documents and witnesses that
can decide whether Continuance custody must mean removing a working root from
Tessell.

## A7 — Barrow Warrant

**Why this battle happens:** Armed seizure contractors are moving to take the
archive before the Tender can compare its two lawful documents.

**Why it opens A8:** Holding the archive through the counterattack preserves
both readings and requires the commander to sign one disposition.

**Briefing**

Secure Barrow's archive forecourt and protect the record room through the
contractor counterattack. Keep the building intact. The master warrant supports
export; the maintenance compact supports local stewardship under recorded
obligations. The evidence team will present both when the site is safe.

**Opening exchange**

- Lead / Petra: “We preserve the archive first. Interpretation comes after the
  people with guns have left it.”
- Impulse / Arne: “The warrant is already an interpretation.”
- Observer / Silas: “Then it can survive being read beside the compact.”
- Dispatch fallback: “Secure the forecourt and protect the Barrow record room.”

**Field exchange — counterattack begins**

- Practical / Teodor: “Contractor markings, no Tender channel. They want the
  archive before we can make a choice.”
- Lead: “Then their urgency is another reason to keep it intact.”

**Aftermath: What the warrant permits**

Barrow supports two lawful outcomes. Export sends disputed roots to reinforce
failing Continuance civil defence and closes Tessellan yards that rely on them.
Countersigned stewardship keeps the machines here under named maintenance
obligations and leaves shortages elsewhere. Neither creates a root or cancels
the other claim. The commander must choose which cost the detachment will own.

## A8a — Export Under Seal

**Why this battle happens:** The commander orders export. Previously established
seizure contractors attack the loading corridor to capture the roots and erase
the Tender's documented chain of custody.

**Briefing**

Hold the archive freight corridor while the export register closes and the
first root is loaded. Attacks will come through announced freight and cooling
approaches. Keep the loading stand operational. Once the register is sealed,
the shipment is lawful and complete; pursuit is unnecessary.

**Opening exchange**

- Impulse / Arne: “The Continuance sent us because people beyond Tessell need
  these machines. We complete the duty.”
- Observer / Silas: “And the record will name every yard that closes for it.”
- Lead / Petra: “Both statements enter the account. Hold the corridor.”
- Dispatch fallback: “Protect the loading corridor until the export register is
  sealed.”

**Field exchange — final approach announced**

- Practical / Teodor: “Cooling road, heavy contact. Last scheduled loading cycle.”
- Lead: “Keep the stand open. We leave with the work complete.”

**Ending: The shipment closes**

The export register is sealed. Continuance settlements will receive inherited
roots their own service works cannot replace. Tessellan fitters watch the same
machines leave the yards that kept them alive. The detachment has fulfilled a
lawful warrant and recorded its full cost instead of hiding it beneath a clean
total.

The crew returns with serviced machines where discipline preserved them and
local replacements where necessity demanded them. Arne calls the shipment duty
done. Silas keeps the closure list attached to the report. Petra submits both.
The account balances only because it refuses to pretend that nothing was lost.

## A8b — Countersign Stewardship

**Why this battle happens:** The commander orders local stewardship. Armed
seizure contractors attempt to capture the archive and force an export before
the alternate disposition is verified.

**Briefing**

Secure the public verification desk and the service-reader station, then protect
the local handover while the Tender countersigns it. The positions are separated;
move as a formation rather than leaving one machine at each. The contractors
oppose the recorded disposition, not the Aurelian detachment's identity.

**Opening exchange**

- Observer / Silas: “The compact is lawful, witnessed and harder than export to
  administer. That does not make it the softer choice.”
- Impulse / Arne: “It leaves our own settlements short.”
- Lead / Petra: “Yes. The signature is mine. We verify it properly.”
- Dispatch fallback: “Secure both verification positions and protect the local
  handover.”

**Field exchange — handover begins**

- Practical / Teodor: “Contractors on the freight road. They expected custody
  to mean possession.”
- Lead: “Today it means responsibility. Hold the handover.”

**Ending: A different form of custody**

The Tender countersigns local stewardship. The roots remain on Tessell, with
the workshops named as keepers and their service history preserved. The
Continuance's need remains real. The detachment has declined to meet it by
closing these yards, and its report must explain what other settlements will
do without the shipment.

Petra carries the signed record home. Silas supplies the argument, Teodor the
operational cost and Arne a formal dissent that remains part of the account.
The crew did not abandon duty. It chose a reading of custody that will demand
work long after the guns are quiet.

## Continuity checks for implementation

- Aurelian equipment remains superior per tonne. Difficulty comes from heat,
  replacement cost, terrain, divided duties and enemy routes, not a hidden
  faction penalty.
- The survey and transfer are optional. No later briefing assumes they occurred;
  only an equipment acknowledgement may change.
- Root Exchange uses sequential reader visits. It does not require simultaneous
  occupation or destruction of all patrols.
- Quarry Receipt requires one evidence site. The exposed second site is an
  optional reward and fuller record.
- Conduit Injunction has one required hold and one optional bypass. Copy must
  never claim that the player rerouted power through an unsupported mechanic.
- The finale opponents are the armed seizure contractors identified at Barrow,
  not unexplained Aurelian allies who suddenly turn hostile.
- Export and Stewardship are exclusive and comprehensible without optional
  evidence. Crew disagreement survives either ending.

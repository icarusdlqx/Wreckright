## Description

**Your company. Your mechs. Your next move.**

Ironmuster is a single-player tactical mech game with real-time combat and a pause
button. On Tessell, the Great Recall has turned working machines into disputed
property. Command the resourceful Linewrought or the advanced Aurelian Stock
through two distinct sides of the same campaign.

- **Pause. Plan. Commit.** Scout through fog of war, coordinate your weapons and
  choose when to call sensors, air support or battlefield repairs.
- **Make each drop count.** Mission tonnage limits and up to five deployment
  seats reward choosing the right machines and pilots.
- **Build your own variants.** Open the Mechlab, fit weapons and ammunition into
  each body section's mounting spaces, balance armour and heat, then save a named
  configuration for campaigns and skirmishes. Compare it with the original Prime.
- **Bring something home.** How you disable an enemy affects what can be recovered.
  Salvage parts, repair immediately with credits and take your revised machine into
  the next mission.
- **Keep your people alive.** Pilots gain experience, develop skills and remember
  parts of their service. Injured pilots miss the next mission; KIA pilots are lost.
- **Choose your battlefield.** Skirmish offers map, faction, force and difficulty
  choices. Explore 16 mech chassis and their histories in the built-in wiki.

### Start here

New commander? Choose **Learn Command**, then start **The Great Recall** or try a
skirmish. Desktop or laptop in fullscreen is recommended. Click or drag to select,
right-click to move or attack, and press Space to pause. Orders still work while
paused. Music and sound effects can be controlled separately in Settings.

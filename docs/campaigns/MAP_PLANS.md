# Campaign map plans

These plans are the authored contract for the six campaign locations. Terrain
controls movement and cover; the scenery family only dresses that terrain.
Coordinates below use map tiles and are deliberately kept away from the edge.

## Linewrought workshop belt

- Player staging: south service court, columns 20–27 / rows 42–45.
- Primary sites: Marker Eleven west of centre; repair gantry in the north-east;
  freight recovery court in the east.
- Routes: the central freight road is fastest and exposed; the west drainage
  lane and east workshop street each reach the north without a one-tile pinch.
- Reinforcements: north freight gate and east service gate.
- Cover: irregular building courts interrupt the main firing lane while leaving
  three-tile doors for the heaviest machine.

## Linewrought recovery cut

- Player staging: south-east ramp, columns 35–41 / rows 41–45.
- Primary sites: recovery cradle in the basin, winch on the north service road,
  and an optional cabinet behind the west scrap screen.
- Routes: the basin road is direct; a west shelf and a dry eastern drain provide
  independent approaches.
- Reinforcements: north road and west dismantling shelf.
- Cover: water and scrap walls shape movement without sealing the basin.

## Aurelian landing apron

- Player staging: numbered southern hardstands.
- Primary sites: tender approach, cargo control and service allocation.
- Routes: the formal centre lane is exposed; two broad ravines reach either
  side of the tender complex.
- Reinforcements: north cargo gate and west service cut.

## Aurelian service terraces

- Player staging: south lower track.
- Primary sites: three sequential readers, main conduit isolator and workshop
  bypass.
- Routes: central ramps link every elevation; the lower service track and east
  switchback remain usable by heavy machines.
- Reinforcements: north relay platform and east lower track.

## Aurelian civic exchange

- Player staging: south formal avenue.
- Primary sites: two custody posts and the Sarn registry court.
- Routes: the avenue gives long sightlines; garden lanes and workshop access
  streets reach both flanks.
- Reinforcements: north civic gate and paired side streets.

## Barrow archive

- Linewrought staging: south-west service road. Aurelian staging: south-east
  forecourt road.
- Primary sites: public archive, two purge controls, freight loading court,
  service reader and public verification desk.
- Routes: freight road, public avenue and cooling lane remain distinct around
  the archive block.
- Reinforcements: north freight gate, east cooling gate and west public road.
- Cover: the archive is a fixed central landmark on every visit; objective and
  approach changes supply the different encounters.

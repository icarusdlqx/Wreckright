# The Hands That Kept It — missions L5-L8

Continuation of [LINEWROUGHT_SCRIPT.md](./LINEWROUGHT_SCRIPT.md).

## L5 — Broken Wreckright

**Why this battle happens:** Kestrel fires on an ejected local pilot and blocks
the recovery crew before dusk, trying to destroy evidence and deny salvage.

**Why it opens L6:** Saving the fixed recovery perimeter preserves the recording
but reveals that Kestrel cut the power trunk feeding the western repair yards.

**Briefing**

Kestrel broke wreckright in Sarn's freight sector. Secure the recording mast
and protect the recovery perimeter until the pickup is complete. Wreckright
gives the ground-holder the wreck and lets surviving pilots go home. We do not
need to kill every guard to keep either half of that rule.

**Opening exchange**

- Lead / Kessa: “We keep this rule because every cockpit may be ours tomorrow.
  Mast and pickup. Bring the evidence home.”
- Practical / Dorn: “They cut the south feed. Expect the lights to die when we
  commit.”
- Dispatch fallback: “Secure the mast and protect the recovery perimeter until
  pickup completes.”

**Field exchange — pickup begins**

- Impulse / Ilse: “They shot at a chute. Tell me again this is paperwork.”
- Observer / Marek: “Save it for the record. The western power trunk has just
  gone cold.”

**Aftermath: A rule worth keeping**

Sarn retains the recording and the recovery team clears the yard. No named
company pilot must be the casualty: the breach concerns another local crew and
what can happen to any pilot. Kestrel has lost the evidence, but its cut to the
power trunk leaves the western repair halls dark.

Ridgeward Assembly can restart the line from the switching yards at Shale.

## Optional — Blackglass Attestation

**Purpose:** Establish the source of copied serial plates before the Aurelian
route reveals the same fact, and reward a risky evidence recovery.

**Briefing**

Blackglass Quarry cut replacement plates for the workshop service network. Hold
the weigh ledger and serial punch long enough to copy them. The dust hides
short approaches and punishes a scattered lance. Preserve the evidence; the
remaining defenders may withdraw.

**Opening exchange**

- Observer / Marek: “Those punches copied numbers, not roots. The ledger should
  tell us who asked and what kept running.”
- Dispatch fallback: “Copy the weigh ledger and serial punch. Preserve both
  sites until the transfer completes.”

**Field exchange — evidence secured**

- Lead: “Names, lift dates, repair yards. This is not a secret factory. It is a
  service chain.”

**Aftermath**

The receipts show workshops sharing the least-disputed serials across different
surviving roots. That history will matter at Barrow. It does not make the copied
records original or settle where the roots should go.

## L6 — The Cold Yards

**Why this battle happens:** The western repair yards cannot operate while
Kestrel holds the Shale switching position and blocks the power transfer.

**Why it opens L7:** Restoring power exposes an archived service address and a
route key for Barrow's master manifest.

**Briefing**

Take the switching position, then hold it through the restart. Ridgeward will
transfer the load once the controls are ours. The high road is exposed; the
lower service steps offer cover but lengthen the approach. When the transfer
starts, Kestrel will try once to take it back.

**Opening exchange**

- Practical / Dorn: “Cold gantries do not repair anything. Take the switch,
  hold the switch, let the electricians be brave indoors.”
- Impulse / Ilse: “Lower steps put us under their long guns. I vote useful.”
- Dispatch fallback: “Capture the switching position and hold it through the
  power transfer.”

**Field exchange — power transfer completes**

- Observer / Marek: “Western yards are drawing load. One old service address
  woke with them: Barrow archive, freight access.”
- Lead / Kessa: “Then we finally know which door matters.”

**Aftermath: Power and proof**

The repair halls relight across the valley. Their recovered service directory
points to Barrow, where the Continuance's master manifest sits beside a local
maintenance compact. The company began by trying to keep four names off a
seizure list. It can now reach the record behind the whole Recall.

## L7 — Manifest Key

**Why this battle happens:** Barrow's archive is sealed behind a contractor
checkpoint. Halloran needs the freight access opened before the Custody Tender
arrives to remove the documents.

**Why it opens L8:** Taking the key and optional evidence reveals the two final
courses: keep the manifest intact under company control or destroy it.

**Briefing**

Open the Barrow freight access and hold the key station. A scout can use the
short service route through the cooling plant; heavier machines can push the
freight road behind container cover. An optional witness cache explains the
local compact, but the key alone opens the archive.

**Opening exchange**

- Lead / Kessa: “Everything since Marker Eleven points through that station.
  We open it, and then the choice is ours.”
- Observer / Marek: “Cooling route is live and narrow. Freight road is loud and
  honest.”
- Dispatch fallback: “Secure the key station. The service route and freight
  road are both viable approaches.”

**Field exchange — optional witness cache found**

- Observer: “Barrow kept both readings. Export under the old warrant, or local
  stewardship under the repair compact.”
- Impulse: “So nobody lost the answer. They just preferred the one with guns.”

**Aftermath: Behind the archive doors**

Barrow is open. The master manifest can be held intact, giving Tessell evidence
and making the company its keeper. It can be burned, denying the Authority its
strongest seizure record while destroying a century of service history with
it. Halloran can get the company through the door. It cannot choose what the
company is willing to carry.

## L8a — Take the Manifest

**Why this battle happens:** The company chooses preservation and must hold the
archive and loading access while Halloran copies and secures the master record.

**Briefing**

Take the archive control and freight access. Hold both while the copy is sealed
and the original is moved into company custody. Kestrel will attack the loading
lane after the transfer begins. Keep the buildings intact. The record, not a
body count, is the objective.

**Opening exchange**

- Observer / Marek: “A copied serial never steered a walker. A surviving record
  can still stop someone rewriting who kept it alive.”
- Lead / Kessa: “Then we keep it. Archive and freight lane.”
- Dispatch fallback: “Secure the archive and loading access until the record is
  sealed.”

**Field exchange — final wave announced**

- Practical / Dorn: “Last push on the freight lane. They want the paper more
  than they want us.”
- Impulse / Ilse: “Good. The paper stays behind us.”

**Ending: Keepers of the record**

The manifest survives under the company's seal. Tessell has evidence that its
workshops maintained the roots the Continuance abandoned, and the Authority
must negotiate with people it first counted as inventory. The record does not
erase copied serials or the shortages beyond Tessell. It prevents either side
from erasing the other.

Sarn's gantries remain lit. The rescued fitters return machines to service,
including captured Stock when the parts can be made to fit. The company leaves
Barrow stronger and more visible than it entered: keeper of evidence, defender
of access and answerable for what the record is used to justify.

## L8b — Burn the Manifest

**Why this battle happens:** The company chooses denial and must reach separated
purge controls before holding the archive core through an irreversible burn.

**Briefing**

Reach both purge controls, then hold the archive core while the burn completes.
The controls expose the lance in short stages; once the second is armed,
Kestrel will commit everything it has left. The purge destroys the seizure
ledger and Tessell's certified maintenance history. There is no clean copy
afterward.

**Opening exchange**

- Impulse / Ilse: “No manifest, no neat column of machines to carry away.”
- Observer / Marek: “No service history either. We should say the whole price
  before we pay it.”
- Lead / Kessa: “We know the price. Arm the controls.”
- Dispatch fallback: “Arm both purge controls and hold the archive core until
  destruction completes.”

**Field exchange — second control armed**

- Practical / Dorn: “Archive temperature is climbing. So is every hostile on
  the road.”
- Lead: “Hold the core. We leave when the record is ash.”

**Ending: A claim without a ledger**

The master manifest burns. The Recall loses its strongest seizure record, and
Tessell's workshops gain time to decide their own future. The same fire takes
certified service histories, witness chains and proof that could have supported
local stewardship. The company has protected the machines by making their past
harder for anyone to prove.

Sarn's gantries remain lit, but every future claim will be argued from scattered
copies and living memory. The company leaves Barrow as the force that made the
choice. Its victory is real, its responsibility permanent, and the empty space
in the archive belongs to Tessell now.

## Continuity checks for implementation

- The rescued people in L2 are local fitters, not one of the named starting
  pilots. Broken Wreckright likewise never requires a named pilot casualty.
- Completing or skipping The Missing Trail changes one briefing acknowledgement
  only. It cannot alter the rescue's availability or outcome.
- The workshop defended in L3 is the same Sarn network whose freight sector is
  visited in L5 and whose western halls relight after L6.
- The Aurelian machine in L4 is advanced but beatable through formation and
  terrain. Dialogue never claims an invisible faction modifier.
- Optional documentary work may enrich L7 and the endings, but the Barrow
  archive itself presents both choices.
- Take and Burn are exclusive. Each ending closes the campaign without requiring
  the optional board to be cleared.
